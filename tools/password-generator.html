How Secure Random Password Generator works

Create a password with browser cryptographic randomness and at least one character from each selected group.

Steps

Choose a length from 4 to 128, select letters, numbers or symbols, generate and copy the result into your password manager.

Example to check

Selecting all four groups at length 20 produces 20 characters containing uppercase, lowercase, a digit and a symbol.

Limits and review

A long unique password is preferable to a short one. This tool does not save passwords, check leaked credentials or know a service's password policy. Avoid shared devices and clear clipboard history when appropriate.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
