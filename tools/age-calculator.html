Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months and days.

How Age Calculator works

Calculate completed years, months and days from a valid birth date to today in your browser's local time.

Steps

Select all three date fields, then calculate. Clear the result before entering another date.

Example to check

From January 31 to February 28 in a non-leap year, the completed-month convention gives one month and zero days.

Limits and review

An anniversary that falls beyond a month's final day is clamped to that final day. This is a calendar convention, not a legal age determination.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
