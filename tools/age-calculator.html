Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months and days.

Date of Birth

Birth years from 1900 through the current year are supported.

How Age Calculator Works

Calculate completed years, months and days between your date of birth and the current local date.

How to Use

Select your birth day, month and year, then choose Calculate Age. Use Clear to reset the fields and result.

Calculation Method

The calculator counts completed calendar months and the remaining days. Monthly anniversaries that fall beyond the final day of a month are adjusted to that month's last day.

Examples and Special Dates

A birth date of January 31 reaches one completed month on February 28 in a non-leap year. February 29 anniversaries are adjusted to February 28 in non-leap years.

Limitations

Birth years from 1900 through the current year are supported. Future dates and invalid calendar dates are rejected. This calculation is informational and is not a legal age determination.

Privacy

Your birth date is processed locally in your browser. The calculator does not send or store your birth date on an external server.
