How Hashtag Suggestions works

Build Unicode hashtag suggestions from the topic words you enter.

Steps

Enter a specific topic, choose 10, 20 or 30 suggestions, generate and remove irrelevant tags before posting.

Example to check

An Arabic topic retains Arabic letters rather than being reduced to empty tags.

Limits and review

Suggestions use word combinations and a fixed suffix list. The tool does not query trend data, measure competition or predict reach. Platform selection does not provide live platform analytics.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
