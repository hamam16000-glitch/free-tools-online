How PDF to Text Converter works

Extract embedded text from PDF pages to an editable text area.

Steps

Load a PDF, extract text, inspect reading order, then copy it or save a TXT file.

Example to check

A four-page text PDF includes text from all four pages in the output.

Limits and review

Scanned pages require OCR. PDF reading order can differ from the visible page, especially with columns or tables. The converter does not infer missing words or restore original typography.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.

Practice: check whether text is extractable

Open the original PDF in a reader and try to select individual words. When text selection works, the document likely contains embedded text objects that the extractor can read. If an entire scanned page behaves like one picture, the tool may produce little or no output; use an OCR workflow instead of assuming the page has no content.

Review reading order

Two-column layouts, tables and footnotes do not always extract in the sequence a human would read. Test the beginning, middle and end of the output. Keep the original file for visual comparison, particularly if you plan to quote the text in another document.
