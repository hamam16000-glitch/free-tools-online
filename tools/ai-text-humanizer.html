How Text Cleanup and Simple Rewrites works

Apply a fixed English phrase list, whitespace cleanup and optional capitalization changes.

Steps

Paste your text, choose a level, apply edits and compare the original with the result.

Example to check

The phrase 'in order to' becomes 'to'; a standalone 'but' can become 'however' at the strongest level. 'Butterfly' stays intact.

Limits and review

This is a rule-based editor. It does not understand meaning, verify facts or make text undetectable. Read each replacement for grammar and tone before using it.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
