Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rotate an image by 90 or 180 degrees?

Yes. You can rotate an image left or right by 90 degrees, or rotate it by 180 degrees.

Can I rotate an image by a custom angle?

Yes. Use the custom rotation control to choose an angle between -180° and 180°.

Can I flip or mirror an image?

Yes. You can flip an image horizontally, vertically, or use both options together.

Which image formats are supported?

You can open JPG, PNG and WebP images and export the result as JPG, PNG or WebP.

Will PNG transparency be preserved?

PNG and WebP can preserve transparent areas. JPG does not support transparency, so a background color is used when exporting as JPG.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image rotation and flipping happen locally in your browser.
