How Image Rotate and Flip works

Rotate an image to a selected angle and flip it horizontally or vertically in the output's visible axes.

Steps

Load an image, adjust rotation and flips, choose a format, create the result and download.

Example to check

Rotating an 80 by 40 image by 90 degrees creates a 40 by 80 result. A horizontal flip then mirrors the rotated image left to right.

Limits and review

Arbitrary angles enlarge the output bounding box. JPEG needs a background for empty corners; PNG and WebP can retain transparency. Editing settings invalidates the old export.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
