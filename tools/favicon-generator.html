How Favicon Generator works
Create PNG icon sizes and a real multi-image ICO file from a source image.
Steps
Upload PNG, JPEG or WebP, adjust crop or fit, generate icons, and download the files together with the provided installation markup.
Example to check
The downloaded favicon.ico and PNG filenames match the filenames referenced by the generated HTML tags.
Limits and review
Small icons lose fine detail. Inspect the 16-pixel preview, use a simple design and place each file at the path shown in your HTML. No domain ownership or hosting configuration is changed.
Where your data goes
Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
