About Rotate PDF

Rotate all or selected pages in a PDF without converting the original pages to images.

How to Use Rotate PDF

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Select all pages or specify custom pages.
  3. Choose the rotation angle.
  4. Create and download the rotated PDF.

Key Features

  • Rotate selected pages
  • 90° and 180° rotation options
  • Preserve original page quality
  • Local browser processing

Privacy & Security

Your files are processed directly in your browser by this tool and are not uploaded to our server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rotate only selected PDF pages?

Yes. Choose Custom Pages and enter pages such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Does rotating reduce PDF quality?

No. The original PDF page content is preserved and only the page rotation setting is changed.

Which rotation angles are supported?

You can rotate pages 90 degrees clockwise, 90 degrees counterclockwise, or 180 degrees.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Processing takes place locally in your browser.
