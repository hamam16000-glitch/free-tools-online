How Rotate PDF Pages works

Add 90, 180 or 270 degree rotations to every page or a selected range.

Steps

Load a PDF, select pages and a rotation, create the result, then check it in a PDF viewer.

Example to check

An existing 90 degree page rotated right again becomes 180 degrees, while an unrotated page becomes 90 degrees.

Limits and review

Rotation changes page orientation metadata without OCR or content editing. Document signatures or interactive features may be affected when a new PDF is saved.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
