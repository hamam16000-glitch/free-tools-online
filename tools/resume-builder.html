How Resume Builder works

Create a resume preview and downloadable PDF from the work, education and skills you enter.

Steps

Choose a template, fill sections, add experience and education entries, then review the full preview before exporting.

Example to check

Empty sections are omitted from the exported PDF, while entered job descriptions appear as plain text.

Limits and review

Content is not verified or automatically tailored to a job. PDF export uses selectable text with embedded fonts and a simple paginated layout; it can differ from the on-screen template. Check page breaks and text size before applying.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
