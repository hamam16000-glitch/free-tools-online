About Resume Builder

Resume Builder is a free online tool for creating a professional resume directly in your browser. Add your personal information, work experience, education and skills while reviewing the result in the live preview.

How to Use Resume Builder

  1. Choose your preferred resume template.
  2. Enter your personal and contact information.
  3. Add your professional summary, work experience, education and skills.
  4. Review your resume in the live preview.
  5. Download the finished resume as a PDF.

Key Features

  • Professional resume templates
  • Live resume preview
  • Work experience and education sections
  • Skills and professional summary sections
  • Instant PDF download

Privacy & Security

Your resume information is processed directly in your browser by this tool and is not intentionally uploaded to our server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this resume builder free?

Yes. You can create, preview and download your resume without paying for the tool.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. Use the Download PDF button to create a PDF version of your resume.

Can I preview my resume before downloading it?

Yes. The live preview lets you review your resume while you create it.

Is my resume information stored?

No. Your resume is created directly in your browser and the tool does not intentionally send your resume details to our server.
