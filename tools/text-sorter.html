Frequently Asked Questions

What is a text sorter?

A text sorter is an online tool that arranges text lines in alphabetical order.

Who can use a text sorter?

Students, writers, developers and anyone working with lists can use this tool.

Is my text stored?

No. Your text is processed in your browser and is not saved.
