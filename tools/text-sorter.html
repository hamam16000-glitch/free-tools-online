About Text Sorter

Text Sorter is a free online tool for organizing multiline text and lists. You can sort lines alphabetically and remove empty lines directly in your browser.

How to Use Text Sorter

  1. Enter or paste your multiline text.
  2. Choose Sort A-Z or Sort Z-A.
  3. Remove empty lines if needed.
  4. Review the sorted result.
  5. Copy the organized text when finished.

Key Features

  • Sort text from A to Z
  • Sort text from Z to A
  • Remove empty lines
  • Organize multiline lists quickly
  • Instant browser-based processing
  • No account or software installation required

Privacy & Security

Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not uploaded or stored on our server.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a text sorter?

A text sorter organizes lines of text into a selected order.

Can I sort text alphabetically?

Yes. You can sort lines from A to Z or from Z to A.

Can I remove empty lines?

Yes. The tool includes an option for removing empty lines from your text.

Can I copy the sorted result?

Yes. You can copy the processed result after sorting your text.

Is my text stored?

No. Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not stored by this website.
