How Text Line Sorter works

Sort complete lines in ascending or descending browser locale order, or remove empty lines.

Steps

Paste lines, choose an operation and copy the resulting list.

Example to check

Sorting 'z', 'a', 'b' ascending produces 'a', 'b', 'z'. Empty-line removal leaves nonempty line content intact.

Limits and review

Sorting is lexicographic, not a numeric sort. Accented letters and case order can vary with the browser locale. Duplicate removal is provided by the separate duplicate-lines tool.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
