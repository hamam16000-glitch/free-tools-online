Frequently Asked Questions

What is a character counter?

A character counter counts the number of characters in your text including spaces and punctuation.

Why use a character counter?

It helps writers check text length for social media, SEO descriptions and online forms.

Is my text stored?

No. Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not saved.
