About Character Counter

Character Counter is a free online tool for measuring characters, words, sentences and paragraphs instantly while you type. It is useful for social media posts, SEO descriptions, forms, articles and other text with length limits.

How to Use Character Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. View the character and word counts automatically.
  3. Check sentence, paragraph and reading-time statistics.
  4. Edit your text and watch the statistics update instantly.
  5. Copy your result or clear the text when finished.

Key Features

  • Real-time character counting
  • Word, sentence and paragraph statistics
  • Reading-time estimation
  • Instant browser-based processing
  • No account or software installation required

Privacy & Security

Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not uploaded or stored on our server.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a character counter?

A character counter measures the number of characters in your text, including spaces and punctuation.

Does it count words too?

Yes. The tool also provides word, sentence and paragraph statistics.

Why use a character counter?

It is useful when writing content with character limits, including social media posts, SEO descriptions and online forms.

Does the counter update automatically?

Yes. The statistics update automatically while you type or edit your text.

Is my text stored?

No. Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not stored by this website.
