How Case Converter works

Convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case using browser Unicode casing.

Steps

Paste text, choose one conversion, and copy or clear the output.

Example to check

The sentence-case option changes 'hello. WORLD' to 'Hello. World'. Title case capitalizes letters at word boundaries.

Limits and review

Capitalization follows simple rules. Names, abbreviations and language-specific title conventions may need manual correction. Languages without letter case will look unchanged.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
