How to Convert Text Case Correctly

Case Converter helps you change the capitalization of text without rewriting it manually. It supports uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case.

1. Uppercase Conversion

Uppercase changes letters to capital letters. For example, Hello World becomes HELLO WORLD. It can be useful for short labels and headings.

2. Lowercase Conversion

Lowercase converts capital letters into small letters. For example, HELLO WORLD becomes hello world. This is useful when standardizing text.

3. Title Case Conversion

Title case capitalizes the beginning of words according to the tool's character-boundary rules. For example, hello world becomes Hello World.

The converter also handles common contractions. For example, don't stop becomes Don't Stop.

This is a mechanical conversion, not a publishing style guide. Some editorial title styles keep short words such as "and" or "the" lowercase. Review important headings before publishing.

4. Sentence Case Conversion

Sentence case changes the text to lowercase and capitalizes the beginning of the text and text following sentence-ending punctuation.

For example, HELLO WORLD. HOW ARE YOU? becomes Hello world. How are you?.

Abbreviations such as Dr. and decimal numbers may require manual review because punctuation does not always indicate the end of a sentence.

5. How to Use the Tool

  1. Enter or paste your text into the input field.
  2. Select the capitalization style you need.
  3. Review the converted text.
  4. Use Copy Result to copy the output.
  5. Use Clear when you want to start again.

6. Important Limitations

Capitalization is not the same as grammar correction. The converter does not rewrite sentences, translate languages or verify proper names.

Languages without uppercase and lowercase forms may remain unchanged. Language-specific casing rules can also require manual correction.

Privacy and Text Processing

The case transformations are performed using JavaScript in your browser. The conversion functions do not need to send the entered text to a server. Review sensitive text before copying it to other apps.
