About Case Converter

Case Converter is a free online tool for changing text between uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case. It helps format text quickly without manually rewriting it.

How to Use Case Converter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. Choose the case format you want to apply.
  3. Review the converted text in the result area.
  4. Try another case option if needed.
  5. Copy the converted text when finished.

Key Features

  • Convert text to UPPERCASE
  • Convert text to lowercase
  • Title Case conversion
  • Sentence case conversion
  • Instant browser-based processing
  • No account or software installation required

Privacy & Security

Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not uploaded or stored on our server.
