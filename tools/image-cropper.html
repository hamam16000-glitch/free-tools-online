Frequently Asked Questions

Can I crop images to a specific aspect ratio?

Yes. Choose Free Crop or preset ratios including 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 and 9:16.

Can I enter exact crop dimensions?

Yes. You can enter exact X, Y, width and height values in pixels.

Which image formats are supported?

You can open JPG, PNG and WebP images and export the crop as JPG, PNG or WebP.

Will transparent PNG images stay transparent?

PNG and WebP can preserve transparency. JPG does not support transparency, so a selectable background color is used.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Cropping happens locally in your browser.
