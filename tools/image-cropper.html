How Image Cropper works

Export a selected rectangle from a PNG, JPEG or WebP image using pixel coordinates or an aspect-ratio preset.

Steps

Load an image, position the crop or edit coordinates, choose the output format and create a new result.

Example to check

Selecting x=0, y=0, width=40 and height=20 exports the upper-left 40 by 20 pixels.

Limits and review

Cropping removes content outside the rectangle. It does not increase image detail. Changing crop or output settings requires creating a new result before download. JPEG uses an opaque background.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
