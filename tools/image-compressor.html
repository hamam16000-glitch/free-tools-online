Frequently Asked Questions

What is an image compressor?

An image compressor reduces image file size to make images faster for websites, apps and sharing.

Does this tool support PNG and WebP?

Yes. You can compress and convert images between JPG, PNG and WebP formats.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded or stored.

Why should I compress images?

Smaller images improve website speed, save storage space and provide faster loading experiences.
