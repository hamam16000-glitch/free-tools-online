About Unit Converter

Unit Converter is a free online tool for converting measurements between supported units quickly and conveniently.

How to Use Unit Converter

  1. Select the measurement category.
  2. Enter the value you want to convert.
  3. Choose the source and target units.
  4. View the converted value.

Key Features

  • Length conversions
  • Weight conversions
  • Temperature conversions
  • Fast browser-based calculations
  • No account required

Privacy & Security

Values entered into this tool are processed directly in your browser and are not uploaded to our server.
