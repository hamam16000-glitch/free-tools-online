How Unit Converter works

Convert supported length, mass and temperature units with explicit factors.

Steps

Choose a category, enter a finite value, choose source and destination units and convert.

Example to check

One international mile is 1609.344 meters, one avoirdupois pound is 0.45359237 kilograms, and 0 Celsius is 32 Fahrenheit.

Limits and review

Displayed values use up to 12 significant digits, so floating-point rounding can remain. Only the units shown in each category are supported. The weight category performs mass conversion, not force conversion.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
