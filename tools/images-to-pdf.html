Frequently Asked Questions

Which image formats are supported?

You can combine JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images into one PDF document.

Can I change the order of images?

Yes. Use the up and down controls beside each image before creating the PDF.

Can I choose the PDF page size?

Yes. You can match the page to each image or use standard A4 or US Letter pages.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The conversion is performed locally in your browser for better privacy.
