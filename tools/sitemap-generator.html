How XML Sitemap Generator works

Create a deduplicated XML sitemap from URLs on the same HTTP(S) origin.

Steps

Enter the site base URL and one page path per line, generate, review the URLs and publish sitemap.xml at an appropriate path.

Example to check

A page listed twice, once with a fragment, appears once. Ampersands in query strings are XML-escaped.

Limits and review

Use up to 50,000 URLs. Lastmod is omitted because the tool does not know actual page update dates. Only include canonical, public pages you want indexed. A sitemap helps discovery but does not guarantee crawling or indexing.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
