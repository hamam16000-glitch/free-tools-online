Frequently Asked Questions

How does PDF to image conversion work?

Each selected PDF page is rendered in your browser and exported as JPG, PNG or WebP.

Can I choose specific PDF pages?

Yes. You can convert all pages or enter custom page ranges such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Can I control image quality?

Yes. You can adjust image resolution and, for JPG or WebP, control the compression quality. PNG is exported losslessly.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion takes place locally in your browser for better privacy.
