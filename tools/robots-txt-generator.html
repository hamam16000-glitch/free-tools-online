Frequently Asked Questions

What is robots.txt?

Robots.txt tells search engines which pages they can or cannot crawl on a website.

Why use a robots.txt generator?

It helps create a correct robots.txt file without writing the rules manually.

Is robots.txt saved?

No, the file is generated directly in your browser.
