How Robots.txt Generator works

Create a user-agent rule and an optional public sitemap reference.

Steps

Enter one user-agent, choose Allow or Disallow and a path beginning with a slash. Copy the result to your hostname's root robots.txt.

Example to check

Disallow: /private/ asks compliant crawlers not to crawl that path. It does not make those pages private.

Limits and review

A project subfolder's robots.txt is not the hostname root file. Robots rules control crawling, not access security or guaranteed removal from search. A sitemap reference does not guarantee indexing.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
