How Writing Pattern Analyzer Works

This tool examines measurable characteristics of written text, including vocabulary variety, repeated words, character counts and approximate sentence length.

How to Use

Paste your text into the analysis box and select Analyze Text. Review the statistics, then copy or download your report.

Vocabulary Diversity

Lexical diversity is calculated by dividing the number of distinct words by the total number of words. For example, a text containing 100 words and 70 distinct words has a lexical diversity of 70%.

Sentence Analysis

Sentence boundaries are estimated using common English and Arabic punctuation marks. Average sentence length is calculated from the word count divided by the estimated sentence count.

Limitations

The tool does not determine whether a text was written by a human or an AI system. It does not calculate AI probability, verify originality or establish plagiarism.

Language Support

Word counting supports Unicode letters and numbers, including Arabic and English.

Privacy

Text analysis takes place locally in your browser. Your pasted text is not submitted to an external text-analysis or AI API.
