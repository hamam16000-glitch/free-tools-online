How Writing Pattern Analyzer works

Measure repeated whitespace-separated words and average sentence length with a transparent, rule-based index.

Steps

Paste text, run the analysis and read the measured statistics before copying the report.

Example to check

Repeating the same word lowers the distinct-word percentage. Longer sentences increase the sentence-length component.

Limits and review

The index cannot establish whether a person or AI wrote text. It is not a probability, accuracy score or evidence of misconduct. Short texts and languages without spaces can produce misleading statistics.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
