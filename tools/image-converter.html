Frequently Asked Questions

Which image formats can I convert?

You can convert JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images to JPG, PNG or WebP.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes. The converter supports batch conversion and can download all converted images in one ZIP file.

What happens to transparent images when converting to JPG?

JPG does not support transparency, so you can choose the background color used for transparent areas.

Does PNG use the quality slider?

No. PNG is exported losslessly, so the quality control is automatically hidden when PNG is selected.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser.
