How Merge PDF Files works

Steps

Add at least two PDFs, arrange their order, merge, then check page order in the downloaded file.

Example to check

Limits and review

Encrypted or damaged PDFs may not load. Document-level bookmarks, signatures, forms and metadata may not survive copying. Use a copy of important signed documents and review the final file.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
