Frequently Asked Questions

How do I merge PDF files?

Choose two or more PDF files, arrange them in the order you want, then select Merge PDF.

Can I change the PDF order?

Yes. Use the up and down buttons next to each selected file before merging.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Your PDF files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our server.

Will the merged PDF keep the original quality?

Yes. Pages are copied directly into the new PDF without converting them to images.
