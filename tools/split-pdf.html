How Split PDF Files works

Separate a PDF after a chosen page, in fixed-size groups or into single-page files.

Steps

Load a PDF, choose the split mode and valid page amount, review planned ranges and split.

Example to check

Splitting a four-page PDF into groups of two creates two two-page files in the original order.

Limits and review

Page numbers are one-based. Encrypted or damaged files may not load. Document-level signatures, forms and bookmarks may not be retained when pages are copied to separate files.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
