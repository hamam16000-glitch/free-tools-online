Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract specific PDF pages?

Yes. Enter page ranges or tap page previews to select exactly which pages you want to extract.

Can I delete pages from a PDF?

Yes. Select Delete Selected Pages, choose the pages to remove, and create a new PDF containing the remaining pages.

Does this tool reduce PDF quality?

No. Original PDF pages are copied directly without converting them to images.

Are my files uploaded?

No. PDF processing takes place locally in your browser.
