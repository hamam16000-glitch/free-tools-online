How PDF Text to Word works

Create a real DOCX document containing embedded PDF text as paragraphs.

Steps

Select a text PDF, convert it, review the extracted text, then download and open the DOCX.

Example to check

Text extracted from successive pages is written as document paragraphs with page boundaries where possible.

Limits and review

This is text extraction, not full document reconstruction. Original tables, images, fonts and layout are not reproduced. Scanned pages require OCR. Check reading order before editing or sharing the Word file.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
