Local QR Code Generator

Create QR codes for links, text, WiFi, email, phone and contacts.

How Local QR Code Generator works

Generate UTF-8 QR images locally for URLs, text, WiFi, email, phone or contact details.

Steps

Choose a content type, fill the required fields, choose a dark foreground on a light background, generate and scan-test the PNG.

Example to check

Arabic text and emoji are encoded as UTF-8. WiFi reserved characters are escaped before encoding.

Limits and review

Long content needs more modules and a larger image. A logo can reduce scan reliability even with high correction. The QR payload, including WiFi passwords, stays in your browser and is visible to anyone who scans the image.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
