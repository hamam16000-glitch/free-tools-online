How CSV to Excel Converter works

Create a real XLSX workbook from CSV fields kept as literal text.

Steps

Choose a CSV file up to 25 MB, convert it, then download the XLSX result.

Example to check

A value such as 00123 stays 00123, and =2+2 stays text rather than becoming an Excel formula.

Limits and review

Automatic numeric and date conversion is intentionally avoided. CSV dialects and text encoding vary; inspect separators, non-English text and row counts in the result. Formula execution is not added by the converter.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.

Practice: test leading zeros and formula-like data

Save these three CSV rows as a UTF-8 file: label,value, PostalCode,00123, and Input,=2+2. Convert the file and open it in Excel or another spreadsheet program. The second row should retain 00123 as text; the third should show the literal characters =2+2, not a calculated result of 4.

Common import problems

CSV files may use semicolons instead of commas, double quotes to surround fields, or unusual text encodings. Inspect a few rows before trusting the export. Because this converter intentionally preserves raw text, calculations require manually converting intended numeric columns into numbers after checking their content.
