How CSV to Excel Converter works

Create a real XLSX workbook from CSV fields kept as literal text.

Steps

Choose a CSV file up to 25 MB, convert it, then download the XLSX result.

Example to check

A value such as 00123 stays 00123, and =2+2 stays text rather than becoming an Excel formula.

Limits and review

Automatic numeric and date conversion is intentionally avoided. CSV dialects and text encoding vary; inspect separators, non-English text and row counts in the result. Formula execution is not added by the converter.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
