How JSON Formatter and Validator works

Validate JSON syntax and indent the original tokens without rounding large number literals.

Steps

Paste JSON, format it or check validation, then review the status message before copying.

Example to check

The integer 9007199254740993 remains exactly that token, and spaces inside quoted string values remain unchanged.

Limits and review

Formatting does not repair invalid JSON or validate a business schema. Duplicate property names remain in the source; ordinary JSON consumers may handle them differently. Never paste credentials you do not want visible on your device.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
