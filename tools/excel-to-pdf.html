How Excel to PDF Converter works

Render worksheet cell values as a paginated PDF table with selectable sheets, page size and orientation.

Steps

Choose an XLSX or XLS file, select sheets, adjust page settings and convert before downloading.

Example to check

A worksheet with an ID column and an amount column becomes a table; wide tables may continue across pages.

Limits and review

Charts, macros, formulas recalculation and original Excel print layout are not reproduced. Cached or stored cell values are used. Latin and Arabic font coverage is included; unsupported font glyphs may still need another converter.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
