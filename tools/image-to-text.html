How Image to Text OCR works

Recognize English, Arabic or mixed text from images using local OCR worker and language files.

Steps

Upload a clear image up to 25 MB, choose its text language and extract. Correct errors in the editable output before copying or saving text.

Example to check

A high-contrast image containing 'TOOL TEST 123' can be recognized as editable text; a photograph with blur may need a clearer source.

Limits and review

OCR accuracy depends on font, resolution, contrast and layout. Handwriting, columns and tables can be unreliable. Worker and language assets are downloaded from this site; the selected image is not sent to an OCR API.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
