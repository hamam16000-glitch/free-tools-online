Frequently Asked Questions

What is OCR?

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) detects text inside an image and converts it into editable text.

Which image formats are supported?

You can use JPG, PNG and WebP images.

Can I copy the extracted text?

Yes. After OCR finishes, you can copy the text or download it as a TXT file.

Does image quality affect OCR accuracy?

Yes. Clear images with sharp, readable text generally produce better OCR results.

Are my images uploaded?

The OCR processing runs in your browser using Tesseract.js.
