About Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Base64 Encoder & Decoder is a free online tool for converting text into Base64 format and decoding Base64 strings back into readable text. It is useful for development, testing and data workflows.

How to Use Base64 Encoder & Decoder

  1. Enter the text or Base64 string you want to process.
  2. Choose Encode to convert text into Base64.
  3. Choose Decode to convert Base64 back into readable text.
  4. Review the converted result.
  5. Copy the result when finished.

Key Features

  • Instant browser-based processing
  • No account or software installation required
