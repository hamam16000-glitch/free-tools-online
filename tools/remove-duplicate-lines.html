How Remove Duplicate Lines works

Keep the first occurrence of each exact text line and remove later identical lines.

Steps

Paste one item per line, remove duplicates, compare counts and copy the result.

Example to check

The lines 'a', 'a' and 'A' become 'a' and 'A'; matching is case-sensitive.

Limits and review

Whitespace is significant and line order is preserved. A blank line can remain once. This does not normalize names, email addresses, punctuation or spelling before comparison.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
