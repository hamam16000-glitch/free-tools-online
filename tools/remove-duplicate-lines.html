About Remove Duplicate Lines

Remove Duplicate Lines is a free online tool for cleaning multiline text by removing repeated lines and keeping unique entries. It is useful for lists, keywords, datasets and other text collections.

How to Use Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Paste your multiline text into the input box.
  2. Click Remove Duplicate Lines.
  3. Review the cleaned result.
  4. Check the updated line information.
  5. Copy the cleaned text when finished.

Key Features

  • Remove repeated text lines
  • Keep unique entries
  • Clean lists and datasets quickly
  • View line information
  • Instant browser-based processing
  • No account or software installation required

Privacy & Security

Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not uploaded or stored on our server.
