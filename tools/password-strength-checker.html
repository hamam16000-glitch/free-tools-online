Frequently Asked Questions

What is a password strength checker?

It analyzes password characteristics and provides an indication of password strength.

Are passwords stored?

No. Passwords are checked locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our server.

What makes a strong password?

A stronger password generally uses sufficient length and a combination of different character types while avoiding easily guessed patterns.

Does a strong rating guarantee that a password is secure?

No. A strength rating is only an estimate. You should also use unique passwords and follow good account security practices.
