How Password Character Checklist works

Check length and character categories without sending the password to a checking service.

Steps

Enter a sample password or generate one, then review the five checks and common-pattern warning.

Example to check

Arabic letters count as letters, not symbols. A mixed 16-character generated password can meet all five checks.

Limits and review

Five completed checks do not prove a password is secure. The tool does not search breach databases or estimate cracking time. Reused and predictable passwords can still pass a character checklist.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
