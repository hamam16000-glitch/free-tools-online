How PDF Text to Excel works

Extract embedded PDF text fragments into a real XLSX sheet with Page and Text columns.

Steps

Select a text-based PDF, convert it, then open the XLSX and check extracted fragments against the source.

Example to check

A fragment 'Page 2' found on the second page appears in a row whose Page value is 2.

Limits and review

Original table columns, cell types, formulas and visual layout are not reconstructed. Scanned PDFs have no embedded text to extract; use OCR first. Text order follows the PDF's stored content order.

Where your data goes

Your selected files and entered content are processed in your browser. Processing does not upload them to a conversion, QR or AI API. The site serves the scripts, fonts and any OCR models needed for the task.
